Why the budget matters here

Every visitor load run asks the scheduler for CPU out of the same 3000m. When there is not enough, the Job sits Pending and the console says which limit stopped it and what is holding the budget, rather than reporting a generic failure. That is the same panel a visitor sees on their own drop.
